About

18 Years of Artistry

The Nail Ladie is the vision of Heather, a seasoned nail artist, lash technician, and hairstylist who has spent nearly two decades perfecting her craft. Beyond the chair, she's also a nail educator and salon graphic designer — passionate about elevating the industry she loves. Every set is a custom creation — designed with you, for you.

Located in the heart of Depoe Bay on the stunning Oregon Coast, our intimate salon offers a one-on-one experience where you're never just another appointment. You're the only one in the chair.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Where is The Nail Ladie located?

The Nail Ladie is located at 531 US-101, Suites K1-2, Depoe Bay, OR 97341, on the beautiful Oregon Coast. We're easily accessible from Lincoln City, Newport, and the greater Central Coast area.

What are your hours?

We're open Wednesday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:45 PM. Closed Sunday through Tuesday. We recommend booking in advance as Heather works one-on-one with each client.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book online anytime at vagaro.com/thenailladie or call us at (541) 992-1887.

What nail services do you offer?

We offer classic manicures ($35), gel-polish manicures ($35), structured gel manicures ($65), Gel-X and sculpted extensions ($110), fills ($70), pedicures (from $35), tiered nail art ($15–$60), French/ombré designs ($15), haircuts (from $35), and Seint Beauty makeup artistry.

How much do eyelash extensions cost?

Classic, hybrid, and volume lash full sets are $150. Mega volume full sets are $175. 2-week fills start at $75, 3-week fills at $85. Mini lash fills are $40. Lash removal is $35.

Is this a private salon?

Yes — The Nail Ladie is an intimate, one-chair salon. You receive Heather's undivided attention for your entire appointment. No rush, no noise, no other clients. It's a truly personalized experience.
