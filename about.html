Meet Heather

The artist behind The Nail Ladie — 18+ years of passion, skill, and coastal creativity.

Your Artist

One chair. One client. One artist. Your appointment is entirely yours.

Heather is the owner and sole artist at The Nail Ladie in Depoe Bay, Oregon. With over 18 years of professional experience in nails, lash extensions, and hair, she brings decades of refined skill to every appointment.

Our Storefront — Suite K
The Nail Station

When you book with Heather, you're not just getting a service — you're getting her full, undivided attention. The Nail Ladie is a private, one-chair salon where you are the only client. No waiting room. No background noise from a dozen other stations. Just you, Heather, and a calm, focused experience crafted around what you want.

From gel manicures and Gel-X extensions to dip powder, nail art, lash extensions, and haircuts, Heather handles everything herself with the precision and care that only comes from 18+ years behind the chair. She specializes in custom designs — chrome finishes, cat eye, intricate stamping, rhinestone art, and one-of-a-kind creations that turn nails into wearable art.

Beyond her work as a nail tech, Heather is a nail educator and salon graphic designer. She teaches and mentors other nail professionals, and designs branding and graphics for salons across the industry. Her Instagram is a gallery of her latest work, tutorials, and creative inspiration.
